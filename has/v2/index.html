00 / Start

One continuous video.

Scrolling is the animation timeline. No click is required to understand the method.

01 / Background

Hard selection deletes evidence.

Importance becomes a binary keep/drop decision. Bridging context can disappear before the MLLM ever sees it.

02 / Rewind

HAS restores every frame.

Low highlight means less emphasis, not deletion. The full stream remains available.

03 / Highlight prior

Scores become a temporal prior.

H(F,q) → Interp → MinMaxNorm. The curve aligns with the entire video timeline.

04 / Token microscope

A frame opens into visual tokens.

The scalar hₜ is copied over the P visual tokens that originate from that frame.

05 / Steering vector

Move the prior into log-space.

V = log(Repeat(h,P)+ε) creates an additive bias aligned to visual-token logits.

06 / Attention steering

Inject gβV before softmax.

Selected cross-attention heads redistribute mass while the backbone MLLM stays frozen.

07 / Output

Summarize with evidence intact.

The model generates with soft temporal guidance rather than a hard frame bottleneck.
